## Purpose & context

- Fabiano works in the AGP (Gerenciamento de Programa & Projetos) area of a private/mixed enterprise
- Colleague João Marcelo also works in AGP
- Other key stakeholders/colleagues: Erika Ruiz, Rafael Garcia, and Agustin (STI)
- The primary initiative is SGPA (Sistema de Gestão de Projetos e Atividades), a web-based project and activity management system
- SGPA is designed to replace individual Excel-based tracking tools ("Card WIN") used by the GIA and STI teams

## Foundational deliverables completed

- Scope document (SGPA_Escopo_v1.0): 45 user stories across 8 epics in 3 delivery phases
- AS-IS process analysis based on a real Card WIN Excel file, identifying 8 critical pain points
- Architecture decisions captured as 6 ADRs
- Master Reference Document (SGPA_Documento_Mestre_v1.0, ~23 pages) consolidating scope, stack, ADRs, design system, Prisma schema, 15 business rules, backlog, roadmap, meeting structures, and Claude Code prompt templates

## Current state

- A master Claude Code prompt and repository initialization sequence are ready to execute
