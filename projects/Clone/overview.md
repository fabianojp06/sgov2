## Purpose & context

- Fabiano is a technology professional working as a requirements analyst, developer, and process analyst
- Primary project: creating a comprehensive digital copy of himself — an AI-powered persona that thinks, writes, speaks, and interacts like him across multiple channels
- The project has four defined phases: (1) AI training on his communication style and personality, (2) visual and voice persona construction, (3) deployment of the digital copy to interact with real people across channels, (4) automated content creation in his style
- Fabiano wants to pursue all four phases and all use cases simultaneously, without a single priority focus
- Identity elements the digital copy should reflect: professional background in tech; direct, objective communication style but relaxed with natural humor and slang; commitment to family and personal faith convictions; passion for football as a Flamengo supporter
- The system prompt for the digital copy was drafted in Portuguese, reflecting his natural language of communication

## Current state

- A complete foundational system prompt in Portuguese has been produced, incorporating the described personality, communication style, and identity
- The prompt contains placeholders for his specific tech stack details, which are still pending
- Immediate next step: Fabiano to gather 5–10 real text samples (messages, posts, replies) so the system prompt can be refined to capture his actual speech patterns and expressions rather than a generic approximation of his described style

## On the horizon

- Refine the system prompt based on real writing samples
- Define and fill in tech stack details (placeholders still open)
- Progress into Phase 2 (visual/voice persona) and Phase 3 (deployment) work
- Establish automated content generation workflows (Phase 4)

## Approach & patterns

- Prefers a broad, parallel approach — working across all goals simultaneously rather than sequencing them strictly
- Communication with Claude is in Portuguese, consistent with the digital copy's target language
- The project is grounded in authentic identity details rather than generic persona construction
